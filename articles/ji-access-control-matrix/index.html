The Self as an Access Control Matrix

A small notation sheet for projection, compression, category objectivity, instinct, boundary, memory, and future self-editing.

I
$$D = P_\phi(W)$$

Data is not the world. It is a projection made through a sensor, a prior, or a designer.

II
$$Z = C_\theta(D)$$

Compression preserves selected differences. A difference survives when it matters enough.

III
$$O_K(W) = \bigcap_{a \in K} C_a(P_a(W))$$

Objectivity for a category is what survives across its compressors.

IV
$$S_{t+1} = F(S_t,\, \Pi_B(X_t))$$

A boundary is not merely a wall. It filters what may enter the next state.
